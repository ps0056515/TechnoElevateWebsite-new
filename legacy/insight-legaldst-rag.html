Building LegalDST: RAG with Verified Case-Law Citations

8 min read · AI Hub · By Innovexce Engineering

Legal research is one of the highest-stakes domains for generative AI. A hallucinated statute or fabricated case citation isn't a UX bug — it's a professional liability. When LegalDST approached Innovexce to build an AI-powered legal research platform for Indian lawyers, the non-negotiable requirement was clear: every answer must be grounded in verified case law.

The challenge: accuracy over fluency

Off-the-shelf LLMs produce fluent legal prose but routinely invent citations. For practising lawyers, that makes generic chatbots unusable. LegalDST needed a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architecture where the LLM synthesises answers strictly from a curated, indexed corpus of Indian case law — with inline references that link back to source documents.

Architecture: RAG with citation verification

We built a multi-stage pipeline: document ingestion and chunking of case-law corpora, vector embedding with hybrid search (semantic + keyword), relevance ranking with jurisdiction and court-level filters, and LLM generation constrained to retrieved passages only. Each generated paragraph carries verified citation metadata — case name, court, year and paragraph reference.

  • Clerk-based authentication for lawyer accounts and firm-level access
  • Razorpay subscription billing for tiered research plans
  • Chat-style UX optimised for legal query patterns
  • Guardrails blocking generation when retrieval confidence is below threshold

Production outcomes

LegalDST launched with subscription billing live, serving practising lawyers who previously spent hours manually searching case databases. The platform demonstrates Innovexce's enterprise AI capability: not just PoC demos, but production-grade LLM products with auth, payments and domain-specific guardrails.
