Building SRE & Observability for Production Microservices

7 min read · DevOps & SRE · By TechnoElevate Engineering

Microservices multiply failure modes. A monolith gives you one log file and one dashboard. Twenty microservices give you twenty deploy pipelines, twenty sets of metrics and twenty potential points of cascading failure. Without deliberate SRE practices and unified observability, production becomes a guessing game.

From monitoring to observability

Traditional monitoring tells you when something is down. Observability lets you ask new questions about systems you didn't anticipate — tracing a slow API call across five services, correlating a spike in error rates with a deployment, or finding the one database query causing tail latency.

We implement the three pillars together: Prometheus metrics for the four golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, saturation), centralised structured logging with correlation IDs, and OpenTelemetry distributed tracing across service boundaries.

SRE practices that stick

SRE at TechnoElevate starts with SLIs and SLOs agreed with the business — not arbitrary uptime targets. Error budgets drive release velocity: when the budget is healthy, ship fast; when it's depleted, focus on reliability work. Blameless postmortems turn incidents into systemic improvements, not finger-pointing.

Production proof: Verizon MyBiz

For Verizon's MyBiz SMB platform, our team operates the microservices backend with proactive monitoring, root-cause analysis and performance tuning — maintaining minimal production incidents across high-traffic wireless account flows. This is SRE as managed service, not a slide deck.
