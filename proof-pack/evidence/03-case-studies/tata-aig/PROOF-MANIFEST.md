### Problem

General insurance claims across multiple lines - automobile, home, marine, travel, personal accident - needed unified digital processing that legacy tools couldn't handle across varied claim types.

### Solution

Built a Vue.js / Vuetify-based claims application covering create-claim, edit, damage assessment and payment processing across all product lines with a clean, modern lifecycle UI.

### Tech stack

- Vue.js
- Vuetify
- REST APIs
- Postman
