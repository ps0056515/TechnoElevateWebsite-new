### Problem
UK corporates pushed suppliers onto 60–120 day payment terms. In a tightening credit cycle, SME suppliers were starved of working capital with no efficient alternative financing option.

### Solution
Designed a two-layer platform: DDRX lets suppliers request early payment on approved invoices via dynamic discounting; DFX routes receivables to the best liquidity source — buyer, Lloyds, or third-party funders.

### Tech stack
- Microservices
- API-first Architecture
- Cloud-native Deployment
