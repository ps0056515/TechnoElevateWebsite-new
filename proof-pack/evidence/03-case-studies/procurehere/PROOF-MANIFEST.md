### Problem

ProcureHere's legacy monolithic procurement platform couldn't scale with expanding multi-tenant enterprise operations - security, maintainability and modern integration needs outpaced the old architecture.

### Solution

Migrated ProcureHere from monolith to cloud-native microservices, preserving the complete procure-to-pay lifecycle (supplier onboarding, RFX, auctions, contracts) with strict multi-tenant data isolation.

### Tech stack

- Microservices
- Cloud-native
- Multi-tenant Architecture
