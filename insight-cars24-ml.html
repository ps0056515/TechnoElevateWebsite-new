In-House Credit Scoring at CARS24 Scale

6 min read · BFSI · By TechnoElevate Engineering

CARS24's used-car lending business was paying heavily for third-party credit scoring APIs — while still struggling to approve new-to-credit applicants who lacked traditional bureau history. The business case for in-house ML was clear: cut per-application costs and unlock a segment competitors couldn't serve.

Alternative data as the differentiator

Traditional bureau scores fail for thin-file applicants. CARS24's advantage was rich alternative data: banking transaction patterns, SMS-derived financial behaviour and platform engagement signals. TechnoElevate built proprietary microservices to ingest, normalise and score this data in real time — eliminating external API dependency entirely.

Engineering for lending volume

The scoring engine runs as part of a broader fintech microservices stack (Saarthi + Simpler platforms), handling CARS24's application volume with sub-second inference. Models are versioned, A/B tested and monitored for drift — standard MLOps practice applied to a high-stakes underwriting domain.

Measurable impact

Significant cost reduction versus third-party scoring, higher approval rates for new-to-credit users, and a fully owned IP asset that scales with CARS24's lending growth. This is the pattern we replicate across BFSI clients: replace expensive black-box APIs with transparent, tunable in-house models.
